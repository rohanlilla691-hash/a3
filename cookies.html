Digital Stewardship

Cookie Policy & Digital Preferences

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•TableAndPlate Privacy Standards

1. Principles of Artisanal Transparency and Digital Privacy

TableAndPlate maintains an unwavering commitment to digital refinement, distraction-free reading, and patron data privacy. We believe your exploration of our ceramic engineering monographs and tableware archives should remain private and unburdened by invasive third-party ad retargeting trackers. This Cookie Policy transparently details our use of essential session storage and local preferences.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical & Session Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the secure operation, cryptographic verification, and rendering stability of our platform. These cookies maintain encrypted SSL/TLS session states, verify commission form submissions against cross-site request forgery, and calculate reading progress percentages across technical treatises. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without impairing platform functionality.

3. Local Storage for Palette and Scenography Preferences

To ensure optimal visual luxury during extended reading of our technical tableware treatises, TableAndPlate utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your preferred interface theme (Salon Dark vs. Salon Light) and last-used Scenography Studio selections. This configuration data resides strictly within your local browser sandbox and is never transmitted to marketing servers.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Analytics

We deploy privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 telemetry (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to assess aggregate website loading latencies, device viewport dimensions, and navigational engagement. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of ingestion, ensuring no individual client identity is ever associated with website visits.

5. Browser Management and Cookie Controls

Patrons maintain complete freedom to review, disable, or delete stored cookies and local storage tokens at any time through web browser preferences (supported in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge). Please note that clearing essential storage may reset your custom theme preferences.

6. Governance Inquiries and Data Stewardship Contact

For questions regarding our cookie protocols or to discuss our digital privacy governance, please contact our data stewardship desk at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone us at +1-888-777-5845.
