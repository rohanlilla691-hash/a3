Atelier Governance

Privacy Policy & Client Data Governance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•Haute Tableware Privacy Standards

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality and Digital Discretion

At TableAndPlate Ceramic Atelier, we hold the privacy, personal discretion, and bespoke commission records of our culinary patrons, chefs, and digital visitors in the highest regard. We operate under strict data minimization principles, adhering fully to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and luxury hospitality privacy frameworks.

2. Scope and Nature of Information Collected

To process bespoke tableware commissions, coordinate private dining consultations, and deliver gastronomic research monographs, we collect essential professional details (such as full name, institutional affiliation, email address, telephone number, and custom service notes). Technical browsing telemetry, including pseudonymized IP addresses and device browser parameters, is gathered exclusively to maintain secure encrypted connections and optimize platform rendering performance.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

Personal data is processed strictly to respond to bespoke inquiries, manage kiln firing schedules, dispatch ceramic specimens, and provide design guidance on table scenography. We never utilize member data for automated behavioral profiling, intrusive tracking networks, or unsolicited commercial marketing campaigns.

4. Absolute Guarantee Against Commercial Data Sale

TableAndPlate enforces an absolute institutional guarantee: we never sell, lease, monetize, or publicly disclose client records or contact lists to third-party marketing syndicates, ad networks, or data brokers. Information is shared solely with essential operational infrastructure partners—such as secure cloud servers and encrypted email delivery systems—under strict non-disclosure obligations.

5. Cryptographic Security Architecture and Data Retention

All digital communications and technical commission transmissions are protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols during transit and AES-256 military-grade encryption at rest. Patron records are maintained securely within isolated databases, accessible solely by authorized atelier design directors.

6. Universal User Privacy Rights and Contact

Patrons maintain full legal rights to review, update, export, or request the immediate erasure of their personal records at any time. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our data stewardship protocols, please contact our Data Governance Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our concierge desk at +1-888-777-5845.
