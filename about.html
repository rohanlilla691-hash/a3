The Ceramic Metallurgy & Scenography of the Dining Table

By Master Ceramist & Gastronomic Scenographer•Haute Tableware Standards
Inside the TableAndPlate Ceramic Atelier: where kaolin clay chemistry, gas reduction kilns, and gastronomic geometry converge to create timeless dining tableware.

At TableAndPlate, our atelier is dedicated to the artistic lineage, materials science, and dining ergonomics of fine ceramic tableware. A plate is not merely a passive vessel for food; it is an active architectural canvas that influences sensory perception, thermal retention, and the visual emotional narrative of every meal.

From formulating pure kaolin and feldspar porcelain bodies vitrified at 1,280°C to engineering hot-drop forged 18/10 stainless steel cutlery and harmonizing linen table scenography, our research monographs and bespoke tableware commissions bridge classical pottery traditions with modern Michelin-starred culinary artistry.

Our Three Core Tableware Disciplines

1. High-Fire Vitrification: We formulate high-density porcelain and bone china clay bodies that achieve near-zero porosity (< 0.05%), maximum chip resistance, and bell-like acoustic resonance.
2. Non-Toxic Feldspathic Glazes: We formulate mineral-rich wood-ash, celadon, and tenmoku glazes that are 100% lead and cadmium free, offering rich tactile depth that never crazes under thermal shock.
3. Gastronomic Scenography: We curate the spatial proportions, negative space ratios, and cutlery ergonomics that transform dining tables into unforgettable sensory landscapes.
